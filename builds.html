Building styles

Same galvanized steel arch system, three profiles. The right one depends on what goes inside.

01

Full Arch

The classic half-circle Quonset. The arch is the structure, so there is nothing to sag or rot. It sheds snow on its own and uses the least steel per square foot, which keeps the price down.

Best for: storage, barns, garages, anywhere the lowest cost per square foot wins.
02

Straight-Wall

Vertical steel walls with an arched roof on top. You keep usable floor space right to the edges, and overhead doors, man doors, and windows fit like they would in a framed building.

Best for: workshops, commercial space, RV and boat storage, anything with doors along the sides.
03

High-Clearance

Tall straight walls before the arch starts, for equipment that does not fit under a low curve. Lifts, stacked pallets, tall farm machinery, racking, all clear.

Best for: farm equipment storage, truck shops, warehouses, anything tall.

Options and finishes

A steel shell is the start. These are the upgrades customers ask for most.

01

Endwalls and doors

Steel endwalls with overhead doors, sliding doors, and man doors cut to fit. Tell us the door sizes and we build the wall around them.

02

Insulation

Spray foam or blanket insulation for heated shops and year-round work space. Ask at quote time and we will include it in the price.

03

Windows and skylights

Natural light without wiring. Skylight panels in the roof and windows in the endwalls keep the interior bright all day.

04

Ventilation

Ridge vents, louvers, and exhaust fans for shops, barns, and livestock. Moves heat and fumes out, keeps air fresh.

05

Foundation ready

Most buildings sit on a level gravel pad. Pouring a slab? We will give you the anchor layout before the concrete truck shows up.

06

Engineered drawings

Need stamped drawings for a permit? We can arrange engineered specs for your municipality. Ask when you request your quote.

Kit or full build, your call

Every style and size above is available as a DIY kit delivered anywhere in Canada, or as a full build where our crew handles everything from panels to final bolt. Kits come with hardware and instructions. Full builds are typically standing in 2 to 5 days.

Not sure which fits? Send your width, length, and what goes inside, and we will recommend the profile and quote both options.
