Buying questions

Is the price on the site the final price?
It is an estimated range so you can budget. Snow load for your area, exact door sizes and delivery distance set the final number, which we send in writing before you pay anything.
Do I pay online?
No. Sending an order request costs nothing. Once you approve the written price, a deposit puts your kit into production and the balance is due before delivery.
What is not included?
Foundation work, building permits, electrical and taxes. We can point you to the right people for each and give you the anchor layout for your pad or slab.
Can I put it up myself?
Yes. The panels bolt together with basic hand tools. Most handy owners finish a garage-size kit over a few weekends with a couple of helpers. If you get stuck, our crew can finish it.
Where do you deliver?
Anywhere in Canada. Delivery cost depends on distance from our Winnipeg base and is included in your written price. Pick your province in the builder to see the estimate.
