Why Quonsets

The smartest steel building you can buy. Here is why.

01

Durable by design

Galvanized steel arch. No rafters to sag, no wood to rot, no shingles to replace. Rated for heavy prairie snow loads and decades of service.

02

Clear-span interior

No posts, no trusses eating your floor space. The full width is usable, park more, store more, work bigger.

03

Up in days

Bolt-together panels, small crew, simple tools. Most Quonsets go from flat-packed to standing in 2 to 5 days.

04

Winter-proof

The curved roof sheds snow naturally instead of collecting it. Built for Manitoba winters, minus forty and still standing.
