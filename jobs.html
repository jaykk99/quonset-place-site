Join the crew

We are hiring quoters and crew workers across Manitoba. Real work, fast pay, no resume needed. If you can show up and work, you can earn here.

Open roles

Two ways to earn with Quonset Place. Pick the one that fits you.

$25/hr

Crew Worker

Build steel buildings. Bolt panels, set endwalls, anchor base rails, finish the details. Outdoor hands-on work on real job sites around Winnipeg and Manitoba. No experience needed to start, but reliability is everything. You get paid after the customer confirms the job is done right.

You need: your own transportation to the site, work boots, and the ability to lift and work a full day.
$/quote

Quoter

Talk to customers, price buildings, and close quotes. You take the incoming quote requests, work out the size and options with the customer, and send the number. Paid per quote you handle, so the more you close, the more you earn. Work from your phone.

You need: a phone, decent people skills, and the ability to learn building sizes and options fast. We teach you the pricing.
