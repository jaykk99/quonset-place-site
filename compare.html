When a Quonset is not the answer

We would rather talk you out of the wrong building than sell you one you regret.

Straight talk

If you need windows and doors all along both long walls, a curved wall fights you. If the space will be finished like an office, with drywall and drop ceilings, a wood-frame shop is easier to finish. If the lot is tight and every foot of width counts, straight walls use the footprint better. And if you only need a secure 40-foot lockup and nothing more, a used container is cheaper than any building we sell.

For everything else, storage, barns, garages, workshops, the Quonset usually wins on price per square foot and wins big on maintenance. If you are on the fence, send us what the building is for and we will tell you straight which way to go.

Comparison questions

The ones people actually ask before they buy.

Is a Quonset cheaper than a pole barn?

For the same enclosed square footage, usually yes. The arch is the structure, so there are no trusses, no posts, and no separate roof to buy. A pole barn can win when you want a big open building with no insulation and you supply the labor, since lumber and carpenters are easy to find locally.

Can you insulate a Quonset?

Yes. Spray foam sticks directly to the steel and is the most common choice for heated shops. Blanket insulation with a liner works too. Tell us at quote time if the building will be heated and we include it in the price.

Does a Quonset need a foundation?

Most sit on a level gravel pad with the base rails anchored down. Pouring a slab? We give you the anchor layout before the concrete truck shows up.

How do Quonsets handle heavy snow?

That is what the shape is for. Snow slides off a steep arch instead of piling onto a flat roof, and the steel shell has no shingles to lose in a windstorm. Every building is sized for Manitoba snow and wind loads.

Quonset kit or full build?

Kits cost less and ship with instructions, and handy owners finish small buildings in a few weekends. Full builds cost more but our crew has it standing in 2 to 5 days. We quote both so you can see the difference.

What about permits?

Most municipalities want engineered drawings for anything with a foundation or over a certain size. We can arrange stamped drawings for your municipality. Ask when you request your quote.
